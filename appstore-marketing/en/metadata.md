## Description (max 4000)

```
Build a family tree of your relatives — fast, without an account and without the cloud. Everything stays on your phone.

Add parents, children, siblings and marriages. Browse a full family chronicle with birth dates, death dates and your own notes.

Start from yourself and grow your tree generation after generation.

WHAT THE APP DOES
• Visual family tree with pan and zoom
• Add people with first name, last name, gender, birth and death dates
• Relationships: parent ↔ child, marriages (with marriage and divorce dates), siblings
• Automatic sibling linking through shared parents
• Full list of people with search by name
• Change the tree root — view your lineage through the eyes of any ancestor
• Notes on every person

PRIVACY FIRST
• Fully offline
• No account, no sign-in, no cloud
• Your data never leaves your device
• No ads, no tracking

WHO IT'S FOR
• People building a family tree for their own use
• Hobbyist genealogists documenting family history
• Anyone who wants to leave their children an organised family chronicle

Download the app and start your family tree today.
```
