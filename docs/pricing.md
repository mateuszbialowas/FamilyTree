## What you get

- Build an unlimited family tree on your iPhone
- Add parents, children, spouses, siblings — relationships are auto-discovered
- Beautiful, hand-drawn-style visualization
- Export and share with relatives; import their trees
- 100 % offline. No internet required after install.
- No account. No cloud. No analytics.
